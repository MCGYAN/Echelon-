About

A Cape Coast firm
for everyday business finance

Rakone Systems has worked with traders, shops, and small companies in Cape Coast and the Central Region since 2011. Our work covers bookkeeping, tax, payroll, stock records, and the software many businesses now depend on.

Some clients only need monthly books. Others ask us to set up QuickBooks, train the cashier on a POS, or check the accounts before a bank or GRA visit. We take the work as it comes and explain it in plain language.

Services

Our services

Accounting work, compliance support, and software installation under one roof.

01

Bookkeeping

We record your sales, purchases, and expenses so the books stay up to date and ready when you need them.

02

Internal Auditing

We review your records and controls to find missing entries, weak processes, or mistakes before they grow.

03

Payroll Processing

Salaries, deductions, and payslips prepared on time so staff are paid correctly each period.

04

Bank Reconciliation

We match your bank statements to the ledger so what the bank shows agrees with your books.

05

Financial Statements

Income statements and balance sheets prepared for management, lenders, or other parties who ask for them.

06

Tax Compliance

Help with filings, registrations, and staying current with GRA so tax season is less rushed.

07

Stock Management

Stock records for shops and distributors so you know what is on the shelf and what has moved.

08

QuickBooks, Software and POS

We install QuickBooks and POS systems, then train your people so they can use the tools after we leave.
